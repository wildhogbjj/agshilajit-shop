# Strategy: Gym-First Outreach

**Status: running in parallel with `strategy-instagram-first.md`, not sequentially.** Both deploy now. Cross-feed continuously, not just at a handoff point: any gym response (yes/no/sample dropped) is a candidate IG content beat; any IG engagement/DM proof gets folded into outreach messages as it happens.

Shared facts (product, pricing, copy, contact info, gym list) live in `shilajit-shared-assets.md` — pull from there, don't duplicate.

## Objective
Convert existing gym memberships and local relationships into in-gym product placement / word-of-mouth sales, faster than organic search or cold social growth can.

## Sequence
1. Pull gym list from `shilajit-shared-assets.md`.
2. For each gym, pick a message variant:
   - BJJ/Wild Hog tie-in exists (Norton Gym WGC) → use **Message A**
   - No BJJ connection, you're just a member (Rise Gym, NoBull Fitness) → use **Message B**
3. Send message. Log response in the tracking table below.
4. On a "yes": drop off sample box + printed lab cert + QR code to shop. Ask if a flyer near reception is possible.
5. After first sale from that gym: ask the buyer for a Google review (feeds local SEO, tracked separately) and ask permission to feature them (feeds Instagram strategy).

## Message A — BJJ/local collab angle
> Hi [name] — I train locally and run [Wild Hog BJJ], and I'm the UK reseller for AG Shilajit, a Himalayan/Altai shilajit resin that's lab-tested at 81% fulvic acid (most UK brands don't disclose theirs at all — usually 40–60%). I'd love to bring a few boxes in for your members to try, or leave some flyers/QR code near reception. Happy to send the lab cert and a sample box over first. Would that be worth a quick chat?

## Message B — no BJJ mention, lab-cert-led
> Hi [name] — I'm a member here and I distribute AG Shilajit, a Himalayan/Altai resin lab-tested at 81% fulvic acid (most UK brands sit at 40–60% and don't publish the number at all). I'd like to leave a few boxes and a QR code near reception, or bring in samples for a couple of the trainers to try first. I can send the lab certificate over before we talk if that's useful. Worth a quick chat?
