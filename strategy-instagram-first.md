# Strategy: Instagram-First

**Status: running in parallel with `strategy-gym-first.md`, not sequentially.** Both deploy now. Cross-feed continuously: engagement/follower proof gets used as social proof in gym outreach messages as soon as it exists; any gym response becomes content here as soon as it happens — don't wait for a "visible following" milestone to start the crossover.

Shared facts (product, pricing, copy, contact info) live in `shilajit-shared-assets.md` — pull from there, don't duplicate.
Content pillars, cadence, and prompt templates for execution live in the existing `wildhog-content-bank` repo — this file is the shilajit-specific plan layered on top of that system, not a replacement for it.

## Objective
Build local (Hertfordshire) audience trust and inbound DM interest ahead of or alongside gym outreach, using content already scoped in the content bank.

## Sequence
1. Post the finalized collab caption (purity-led, 81% fulvic acid, pinned comment linking to shop) — already drafted in `wildhog-content-bank`.
2. Follow with 3–5 posts cycling through: lab-cert reveal, "how to check your shilajit is real," a training-day energy/recovery post, a customer feedback repost (once available).
3. Geotag Hertfordshire locations (WGC, Hatfield, Stevenage) and use local strength/BJJ hashtags on each post.
4. Track follower growth and DM inquiries weekly.
5. Once there's a visible local following or a few unprompted DMs, fold that into gym outreach messaging ("we've already got local people using it") — see `strategy-gym-first.md`.

## Success metric
Noticeable local engagement (comments/DMs from accounts that are clearly Hertfordshire-based, not generic) within 4 weeks. Feed any of it into gym outreach as it comes in, not as a batched milestone.
