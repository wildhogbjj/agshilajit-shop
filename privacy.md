## What Information We Collect

When you place an order through our order form, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Delivery address
- Order details (quantity, delivery method, delivery instructions)

## Why We Collect It (Legal Basis)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

- **Performance of a contract** — to process, confirm, and fulfil your order, and to send you a payment link and delivery updates.
- **Legitimate interests** — to maintain business records, respond to queries, and prevent fraud, where this does not override your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — to keep records required for UK tax and accounting law.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

## How Long We Keep It

We retain order and customer information for **6 years** from the date of your order, in line with UK HMRC record-keeping requirements for business transactions. After this period, your data is securely deleted.

## Who We Share It With

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with:

- **Formspree**, our order-form processor, which transmits your submitted details to us by email. Formspree is based in the United States; your data may be transferred outside the UK/EU. Formspree's Standard Contractual Clauses and privacy practices are described at https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
- Delivery couriers (e.g. Royal Mail), solely to fulfil delivery of your order.

No payment details are collected or stored on this Site — payment is arranged separately and manually after your order is received.

## Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data, where there's no legal reason for us to keep it
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we use your data
- **Portability** — request your data in a portable format
- **Object** — object to our processing of your data, including for direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram (above). We will respond within one month.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**: https://ico.org.uk

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted on this page.
