Product Information

Ingredients, Dosage & Safety

Ingredients

100% purified Shilajit resin (Asphaltum punjabinum). No fillers, additives or excipients.

Directions for Use

Take a pea-sized portion (approx. 300–500mg) once or twice daily, dissolved in warm water, tea or milk. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Net Quantity

30g resin. Approximately 60–100 servings per jar.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place below 25°C, away from direct sunlight. Replace the lid tightly after use.

Warnings

  • Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.
  • Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
  • A food supplement should not be used as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle.
  • Not suitable for children. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking any medication or under medical supervision, consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.
  • Discontinue use and seek advice if you experience any adverse reaction.
